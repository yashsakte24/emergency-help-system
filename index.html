🩹 Basic First Aid

🩸 Cuts & Bleeding

Apply gentle pressure with clean cloth or gauze and seek medical help for serious bleeding.

🔥 Burns

Cool the burn under clean running water for about 20 minutes. Do not apply ice directly.

😵 Fainting

Help the person lie down safely and seek medical assistance if they do not recover quickly.

🫁 Breathing Emergency

Call emergency services immediately and follow instructions from the emergency operator.

This project helps community members quickly access important emergency services and basic first-aid information. It can be useful during accidents, medical emergencies, fire incidents and other urgent situations.
